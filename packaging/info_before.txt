Behavython — Version 0.9.9rc10 (PyTorch Edition)

========================================================
1. DEEPLABCUT & PYTORCH ENGINE NOTICE
========================================================
This installer bundles Behavython with PyTorch and
DeepLabCut 3.0+.

If your lab requires older DeepLabCut 2.3.x (TensorFlow):
Do NOT use this standalone installer. Instead, follow
repository instructions to install 'behavython[tf]'
via pip in a dedicated Conda environment.

========================================================
2. HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS & GPU ACCELERATION
========================================================
- Minimum GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060, RTX 2080,
  or equivalent.
- Dedicated VRAM: 12 GB VRAM (minimum recommended
  for DeepLabCut / SuperAnimal deep tracking).
- NVIDIA Driver: Version 528.00 or higher (driver > 528
  required for bundled CUDA 12 runtime).
- Operating System: 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11.

[CPU Fallback]
Behavython will function on CPU-only machines, but
video tracking and neural network analysis will be
significantly slower without GPU acceleration.
